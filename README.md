## 🛠️ Common Troubleshooting

Most issues involve microphone settings or system permissions.

*   No text appears: Check if you muted your microphone. Ensure the app has permission to use the microphone in your Windows Privacy settings.
*   App runs slowly: Close background programs that use a lot of memory. Open-dictate needs significant power to process speech.
*   Text errors: Move closer to your microphone. Background noise interferes with the accuracy of the software. Speak at a steady pace.
